Learn re-entry physics

Sea Turtle is a self-study lab: run a transparent 2-DOF entry model, read what the numbers mean, then push into researcher sweeps and CSV export when you are ready.

How to use this tool

  1. Pick a mission preset below (or keep the LEO default).
  2. Stay in Learn mode first — fewer knobs, guided story after each run.
  3. Change one input at a time and re-run. Watch Max G, Heat, and β.
  4. Open glossary terms when a symbol or limit is unfamiliar.
  5. Switch to Research for custom diameter sweeps and data export.

Nothing leaves your browser. Results are conceptual — not for flight certification.

      Engineer's recommendations

        Design philosophy

        • Physics-informed first-order models over black-box solvers.
        • Transparency and clarity in assumptions.
        • Modular design for easy extension and reuse.
        • Emphasis on insight and trade-space exploration.

        STRIDE is intended to complement higher-fidelity tools, not replace them.

        Intended use

        • Conceptual vehicle design studies.
        • Self-study for high-speed aero, thermo, and entry.
        • Propulsion and mission trade analysis.
        • Educational and research projects.

        Not intended for flight certification, FAA Part 450 licensing, or detailed component design.

        Disclaimer. STRIDE is a personal engineering project developed for personal learning, career, and conceptual purposes only. Results should not be used for operational or safety-critical applications.
